Dual-QK: Sharp Queries and Flat Keys for Prunable 2-bit KV Caches

Sunjoo Whang1, Jungjun Oh1, Minsung Kim1, Dongho Seo2, Jisu Shin2, Gregory Kielian3, Hoi-Jun Yoo1, Sangjin Kim2,†
1KAIST · 2GIST · 3Google Research · †Corresponding author
arXiv preprint, 2026
arXiv PDF

Summary

Long-context inference is limited by the size of the KV cache. Two common ways to shrink it pull against each other: rotation-based INT2 key quantization spreads energy evenly across channels, while query-channel pruning needs that energy concentrated in a few channels.

Dual-QK resolves this with a pair of non-orthogonal transforms built from calibrated statistics. The query side becomes sharp, so pruning removes little of it, and the key side becomes flat, so 2-bit quantization loses less.

Key Results

  • 6.8× KV cache compression at 128K context
  • Up to 3.75× decoding throughput in an SGLang implementation
  • Outperforms OSCAR on most tasks at 40% query-channel sparsity, across four models
